# Privacy Policy

Windows Virtual Desktop Helper (Virtual Desktop Helper) works locally on your computer. It does not automatically collect or send personal information, usage data, or diagnostics to the developer or third parties. It has no analytics or tracking. Settings are stored locally.

Diagnostic messages are written to a local, size-limited `app.log` in the configuration directory. The app does not upload logs or settings. If you choose to share them in a GitHub issue, review them first. GitHub and other external sites you choose to open have their own privacy policies.

Questions: [GitHub project](https://github.com/dankrusi/WindowsVirtualDesktopHelper).
